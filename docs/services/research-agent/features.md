# Research Agent

When AI services disagree, you need to know. Most tools blend their answers into one. This one maps every contradiction.

## The Problem

Ask an AI a question and you get one answer. It might contradict what another service would say, but you will never know -- you only heard from one. The people who bother to check routinely find contradictions on dates, disagreements about causality, and facts only one service surfaced. Those contradictions are where the real insight lives. But finding them by hand takes longer than the research itself.

## Use Case: Cross-Referencing a Workplace Health Policy

Your company is finalizing a new remote work policy. You type your question into IntexuraOS.

Before your question reaches any AI service, the system analyzes it and builds a research plan -- identifying the subject area, setting the time scope, generating key sub-questions, and specifying what source types to prioritize. Each service will receive this plan alongside a sharper version of your question.

The system creates a draft for your review: which services were selected, what the refined question looks like, any material you attached. If the system misidentified the domain or selected the wrong services, you catch it here. You approve the draft, and only then does the research begin.

Multiple AI services receive your question simultaneously. Each returns its own report with sources and citations. One emphasizes long-term studies on isolation risks. Another highlights productivity data that complicates the narrative. A third surfaces a comprehensive review the others missed.

Once the responses are in -- even if one service failed -- the system runs a structured conflict analysis -- identifying each topic where services reached different conclusions, naming which ones are involved, and rating the severity from minor discrepancies to major contradictions. The combined report weaves these perspectives together: where the services agreed, where they disputed, and what only one found.

You open each service's original report to check their reasoning. When satisfied, you share the research as a public page with a generated cover image. It also exports to Notion: the combined report as the main page, each service's report as a child.

## How It Helps

### Conflicts Detected, Not Buried

Most tools that consult multiple AI services hand you a blended summary where contradictions disappear. This one runs a separate analytical pass after the responses arrive -- comparing conclusions topic by topic, naming the services involved, and rating the severity. A minor date discrepancy is flagged differently from a fundamental contradiction about whether a treatment works. Because every service received the same structured research plan, their responses are directly comparable -- making the conflict analysis meaningful rather than superficial.

### Your Question, Rebuilt

The system does not pass your question along unchanged. It constructs a research plan: the topic area, key sub-questions, what source types to prefer or avoid, the relevant time window, and your region. Each AI service receives this structured context so their responses are focused -- and comparable enough for the conflict analysis to work.

### Add Your Own Sources

Attach up to five pieces of supporting material -- articles, notes, previous research -- and the system includes them in every service's query. Your existing knowledge informs the research from the start.

### Choose Your Services in Plain Language

Say "use the two strongest" or name specific services. Each service contributes one perspective, so you are not paying for duplicate answers.

### Share and Export

Completed research generates a public URL with an AI-created cover image. Unsharing removes the page and its media. Research also exports to Notion: a main summary page with each service's full report attached beneath it. If your conclusions change later, you can expand completed research by adding more services or new material.

## Key Benefits

- **Structured conflict detection** -- Disagreements identified by topic, rated by severity, attributed to specific services
- **Cost tracking per service** -- Usage and cost recorded and displayed for each service
- **Review before you spend** -- The draft step lets you verify the plan, catch misclassifications, and approve before any service is queried
- **Resilient to partial failure** -- If one service fails, the rest complete; you choose whether to proceed or retry
- **No personal AI keys required** -- Users without their own AI service credentials can research using the platform's shared access
- **Safe to retry** -- Retrying failed research does not create duplicates
- **Notification on completion** -- Research can take minutes; a WhatsApp message arrives when results are ready so you do not have to wait

## Limitations

- **Results arrive together** -- No streaming; the combined report arrives once all services respond
- **Up to five services per research** -- Balances breadth against cost and response time
- **Up to five attached sources** -- Each limited to 60,000 characters
- **No editing after completion** -- Finished research can be expanded with more services or deleted, but not modified in place
- **One model per service** -- Cannot query two models from the same service in one research
- **Single Notion export** -- Re-exporting requires deleting the previous export
